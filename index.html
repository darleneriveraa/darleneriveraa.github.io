Darlene Rivera

Social Worker📚

My name is Darlene Rivera and I am a Licensed Social Worker with over ten years of dedicated experience. I support individuals and families through life’s most difficult transitions. My practice centers on trauma and build judge-free spaces for vulnerable youth and coordinate family services. I studied and trained for many years to specialize in mental health. My years of experience have showed successful results with patients that required intensive counseling. In my current role at a mental health clinic, I have a proven track record of 50+ client caseload. I coordinate care plans and levels of specialized care. I believe with active listening, reassurance, and building genuine relationships can improve healing for those individuals with dangerous trauma. I create studies and assessments for different levels of trauma. Majority of my caseload, I care for individuals who have been incarcerated for substance abuse or possession of drugs. This study falls back with childhood trauma and neglect. Outside my work, I like to hike, bake brownies, and volunteer at my local detention center. My personal passions include family time to level my professional career. My commitment to my community has shaped me to have compassion for those who are struggling with help from my professional skills.
